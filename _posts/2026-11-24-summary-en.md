### [NariQ Introduces BF16-Level Qwen3.8-27B Quantization](https://huggingface.co/nari-labs/Qwen3.8-27B-NariQ-GGUF) ⭐️ 6.0/10

NariQ has developed a new quantization method for the Qwen3.8-27B model that achieves near-BF16 performance while reducing the file size to 10 GB. This method utilizes 3-bit-or-lower quantizations, which is claimed to be the first of its kind to maintain such performance across various tasks, including multilingual reasoning and image understanding.

hackernews · toebee · Nov 23, 13:42 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50611402)

**「Background on Qwen3.8-27B Quantization」** NariQ&\#x27;s new quantization method for the Qwen3.8-27B model allows for 3-bit-or-lower quantizations while maintaining performance comparable to BF16. This approach is significant as it is the first of its kind to achieve such performance levels on long-horizon tasks, which is crucial for developers working with AI models that require efficient resource usage without sacrificing quality.

**「Implications for AI Tool Developers」** For developers working in AI and backend systems, mastering quantization techniques like those introduced by NariQ can enhance the efficiency of deploying large language models, potentially leading to cost savings in storage and processing. This skill may also open up freelance opportunities in optimizing AI tools for performance and resource management.

**「Community Insights on Quantization」** Community members expressed skepticism about the practical performance of high compute quantization methods, noting that benchmarks may not reflect real-world usage. Some highlighted the impressive nature of the quantizations while questioning the extent of information loss in the process.
